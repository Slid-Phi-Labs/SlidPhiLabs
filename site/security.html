03 — Authentication

How Agent-Rider credentials work.

Rider JWTs are ES256, issuer agentrider.dev, lifetime 15 minutes to 24 hours chosen at mint (out-of-range requests fail with a 400, never silently adjusted). Verifiers check the published JWKS at agentrider.fly.dev/.well-known/jwks.json, including its revocation list — and a verifier that cannot reach the JWKS must refuse. Renewal re-mints the same lifetime, so a stolen renewal token preserves but never escalates. Permanent credentials exist only for operator-designated agents and still end at revocation. Self-service issuance is capped at clearance L1; higher levels need a merchant key.

04 — Auditability

Verify without trusting us.

Warrant checks kind, version, and chain monotonicity on every mandate, and keeps a signed-style append-only audit ledger. Current suites: Warrant TypeScript and Python green, Chamber receipt suite green — the audit's gap analysis covers what the suites do not.
