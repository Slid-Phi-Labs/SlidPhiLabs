Chamber

Every action leaves a receipt.

Chamber attests agent actions with cryptographic receipts your auditors can verify without trusting us — including across key rotations.

01 — How you pay

Two rails: commercial tiers, enterprise agreements.

Chamber runs on commercial tiers (see usage), billed by card through Stripe with the standard 24-hour refund window (see refunds). Machine callers settle per receipt through the tollkeeper chamber-seal toll ($0.02 per seal) instead of a seat. Regulated desks that need custom retention, rotation policy, or volume terms take an enterprise agreement with DPA (see security): corey@slidphilabs.com.

02 — Proof before purchase

Audit in progress, receipts today.

An independent review is attempting signature forgery, key-rotation abuse, and replay attacks against receipt verification right now. It delivers a vulnerability report plus a publishable attestation letter. Until then: v1.2.1 is the current release, every receipt verifies against the published JWKS with revocations enforced, and your pilot's receipts are yours to keep and check.
