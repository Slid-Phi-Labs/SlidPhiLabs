Warrant

Give every agent a ceiling.

A mandate states what an agent may spend. Kind, version, and chain are checked before the call runs — over ceiling means refused, and the refusal is ledgered.

01 — How you pay

Two rails: metered calls, merchant keys at scale.

Verification calls meter by usage on your lab account (see usage), billed by card through Stripe with the standard 24-hour refund window (see refunds). High-volume desks move to a merchant key with higher clearance and volume terms under an annual agreement: corey@slidphilabs.com. Unpaid calls refuse machine-readably — never silently.

02 — Proof before purchase

Verify a mandate without trusting us.

Kind, version, and chain-monotonicity checks run locally against the same rules our hosts enforce. An independent security review covering ceiling bypass and race conditions is in progress; its report and attestation letter publish here.
