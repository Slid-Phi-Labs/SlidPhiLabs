01 — How you pay

One rail: metered usage on your lab account.

Compression jobs meter per GB (see usage). Card billing runs through Stripe; every charge carries the standard 24-hour refund window (see refunds). Unpaid calls refuse with a machine-readable payment-required error — free paths are never silently metered. Enterprise volume goes through an annual agreement: corey@slidphilabs.com.

Premium: the binary stays free GPLv3. Closed use ships under a commercial grant — terms on request.
