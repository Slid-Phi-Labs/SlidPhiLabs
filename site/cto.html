For CTOs

Your agents have a budget. Enforce it.

Three questions keep engineering leaders up at night: what can an agent spend, what does the context bill look like, and can we prove what happened. One control answers each.

01 — Liability

Warrant refuses over-ceiling calls.

Every mandate carries a ceiling that is checked — kind, version, chain — before the call runs, not after the invoice arrives. Over ceiling means refused with a machine-readable reason, and the refusal itself lands in an append-only audit ledger. Set the ceiling per workflow; the agent cannot negotiate with it.

02 — Spend

PERsiST stops re-sending context you already paid for.

Instead of concatenating full files into every prompt, context starts empty and folds forward under a token budget. Fewer input tokens per turn, same task completed — measured per run by our evaluation harness, not estimated.

03 — Proof

Chamber and Agent-Rider keep signed receipts.

Identity is a short-lived ES256 credential; every gated action leaves a receipt that verifies against a public key list — including the revocations. Your auditors check the receipts, not our word. The full control list is on the security page, with a DPA for enterprise agreements.

04 — Start

A 30-day pilot on one workflow.
