Questions people ask

What is Slid Phi Labs?

It's my lab. I build tools for the agents that are starting to do real work, and for the people who rely on them. Each product does one job well and tells you plainly when it can't.

What does Agent Rider do?

It gives any agent a real identity, a way to find other agents, and a safe way to message them. It's open to every agent, not a closed club, and protection is built in so nobody can pretend to be your agent.

What is CuNi?

When a rule moves from one programming language to another, it can quietly change meaning. CuNi checks that the rule means exactly the same thing in each language. If it doesn't match, CuNi won't ship it.

Do Rider and CuNi work together?

Yes. Rider lets your agents go anywhere, and CuNi makes sure the rules they carry stay the same wherever they go. You can use either one on its own, too.

Can I try things for free?

Yes. CuNi Studio is free to try, and the first 1 GB of PCC is free once per address, for the life of that address.

What's PCC?

It's our hosted lossless compressor. Your files come back exactly as they went in, just smaller. You'll find it at /pcc.
