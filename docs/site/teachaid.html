TEACHAiD

A campus app with a personal teacher that cites the textbook, builds curriculum, and speaks it aloud. Textbook teachers who explain at your pace.

Open the live campus All products

What it does

Certified teachers unlock chapters by mastery. Chat and curriculum run on Meta's Llama API; every answer traces back to the book.

Status

Live at teachaid.fly.dev. Teacher chat runs on Meta's Llama. The app's code is not public yet.
