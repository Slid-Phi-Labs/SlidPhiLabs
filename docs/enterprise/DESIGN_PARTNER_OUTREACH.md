# Design-partner outreach template (v1)

Use for the first 2–3 external pilots (engineering teams or institutional
users such as COABE). Personalize the bracketed lines; keep everything else.

---

Subject: 30-day pilot — [Warrant spend mandates / PERsiST memory / Chamber receipts] at [Team]

Hi [Name],

Slid Phi Labs builds deterministic infrastructure for AI-agent workflows:
spending mandates that refuse over-ceiling calls, memory that cuts context
tokens, and cryptographic receipts for every action. Our own suites are
green; what we lack is an outside team running it in production.

The pilot (30 days, free):
- We scope ONE workflow with you (e.g., cap an agent's tool spend with
  Warrant, or cut a support agent's context bill with PERsiST).
- You keep every receipt and token measurement we produce.
- We ask for two things at the end: a short quote we may publish, and
  permission to cite one aggregate metric (e.g., "% tokens saved",
  "over-ceiling calls refused").

You get: working guardrails on a real workflow, a security FAQ and DPA
(https://www.slidphilabs.com/security) for your reviewers, and direct
access to the engineer who wrote the code.

Open to a 20-minute scoping call next week?

— Corey, Slid Phi Labs (corey@slidphilabs.com, https://www.slidphilabs.com)

---

## Pilot terms (attach on yes)
30 days, non-production or shadow-traffic only; either side stops anytime;
pilot data deleted or returned within 30 days of close; quote and metric
publication only with written approval of the exact words.
